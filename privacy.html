Privacy Policy for Job Tracker LN

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Job Tracker LN is a free, privacy-first browser extension designed to help job seekers save and manage LinkedIn job listings directly in their own Google Sheet.

We believe your personal job search data belongs solely to you. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to any external server or third party.

1. Single Purpose Declaration

The sole purpose of Job Tracker LN is to allow users to capture publicly visible job information (such as job title, company name, location, and URL) from LinkedIn job postings and record them into a private Google Sheets document owned and controlled by the user.

2. Data We Access & Storage Location

Data Type Purpose Storage Location
Job Details (Role, Company, Location, URL) Extracted only upon clicking "Save Job" or pressing Alt+S. Appended directly to your personal Google Sheet.
Sheet ID & Settings Knows which user-owned Google Sheet to write to. Stored locally via chrome.storage.local.
URL Deduplication Cache Prevents accidental duplicate saves. Stored locally via chrome.storage.local.
Google OAuth Token Authorizes requests directly to Google Sheets API v4. Managed securely by Google via chrome.identity.

3. 100% Client-Side Architecture

  • No Backend Server: The extension operates entirely within your browser. There is no middleman server or external database.
  • Direct to Google: All API communications occur strictly between your browser and official Google APIs (sheets.googleapis.com).
  • Data Ownership: You own the Google Sheet. You can edit, export, or delete your data at any time.

4. Telemetry, Tracking & Cookies

  • No Analytics: No tracking SDKs (Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, etc.) are included.
  • No Advertising: We do not display ads or monetize data.
  • No Cookies: We do not track cross-site cookies.

5. User Controls & Revocation

You can revoke the extension's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions. You can also export your complete job data to CSV with 1 click in the extension popup.

6. Contact

For questions or source code verification, visit the official repository: https://github.com/Himanxusharma/job-tracker-ln.
